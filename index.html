Frequently asked questions

We're not technical — who hosts this?
Anyone comfortable following a getting-started guide can run it on a small VPS. It's a single self-contained app — no separate services to wire up, no dedicated ops person required.
What happens if we migrate off Facebook groups or spreadsheets?
You bring your member list and start posting events and content immediately — there's no forced multi-step migration tool, just a place to move your community's activity to as you're ready.
Who owns our members' data?
You do. It lives in your own self-hosted database, not a vendor's multi-tenant platform. Nothing is shared with or sold to a third party.
What if the volunteer running this quits or hands it off?
Because it's self-hosted on infrastructure your organization controls, handoff is the same as handing off any other admin account and server access — not a vendor relationship that has to be renegotiated.
