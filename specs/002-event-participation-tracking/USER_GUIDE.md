# User Guide: Participation & Photo Features

## Overview
This guide explains how attendees can indicate participation intent, access event photos, and manage their tickets.

## Indicating Participation Status

### RSVP to Events
On any event detail page, you'll see participation options:

#### For Free Events
- **Yes, I'm Going**: Instantly registers you and issues a ticket
- **Maybe**: Records your interest without commitment
- **No, I Can't Go**: Declines the invitation

#### For Paid Events
- **Yes, I'm Going**: Available after purchasing a ticket
- **Maybe**: Shows interest; auto-upgrades to "Yes" when you buy a ticket
- **No, I Can't Go**: Declines without purchasing

### Changing Your Status
- Click the participation toggle anytime
- Your status updates immediately
- Organizers see updated counts in real-time

## Multi-Person Tickets

### Purchasing for Multiple Participants
Some products (hotel rooms, group packages) cover multiple people:

1. Add product to cart
2. During checkout, you'll see **"Participant Details"** form
3. Fill in information for each person:
   - Full name (required)
   - Email address (required)
   - Phone number (optional)
   - Additional info (dietary restrictions, shirt size, etc.)
4. Complete purchase
5. All participants receive confirmation

### Viewing Participant Details
In your ticket view:
- See all participant names listed
- Each participant's contact info visible
- Organizers see this info when scanning your ticket

## Accessing Event Photos

### Who Can View Photos?
Only verified attendees who:
- Have a ticket for the event
- Had their QR code scanned at entrance
- Photos are available after event completion

### Viewing the Gallery
1. Navigate to event detail page
2. Click **"View Photos"** (only appears if you attended)
3. Browse photo gallery with thumbnails
4. Click any photo to view full size

### Photo Access Security
- Each photo URL is time-limited (1 hour)
- URLs are signed with HMAC for security
- Can't share links with non-attendees
- Refresh page if images expire

### Downloading Photos
- Right-click on full-size photo → "Save Image As"
- Photos are served in WebP format (high quality, small size)
- No limit on downloads during access period

## Managing Your Tickets

### Accessing Tickets
- Tickets sent to email after purchase
- View all tickets: **Dashboard** → **My Tickets**
- Each ticket has unique QR code

### Ticket Information
Your ticket displays:
- Event name and date
- Product purchased
- QR code for scanning
- Ticket UUID (backup for manual entry)
- Purchase confirmation number

### At the Event
1. Open ticket on your phone or print it
2. Show QR code to scanner at entrance
3. Wait for green checkmark confirmation
4. Ticket marked as "attended"

### Troubleshooting Tickets

#### Can't Find Ticket Email
- Check spam/junk folder
- Search inbox for event name
- Log in to dashboard to view tickets

#### QR Code Won't Scan
- Increase screen brightness
- Ask staff to enter ticket UUID manually
- Show confirmation email as backup

#### Wrong Ticket Information
- Contact event organizer through platform
- Include ticket UUID in message

## Free Event Registration

### How It Works
1. Find a free event (shows **€0.00** price)
2. Click **"Register Now"** or indicate participation as "Yes"
3. Ticket issued immediately without payment
4. Receive confirmation email with QR code

### Free vs. Paid Tickets
- Free tickets work identically to paid ones
- Same QR code scanning process
- Same access to event photos
- Shows "Free Ticket" label in your dashboard

## Sales Period Restrictions

### Pre-Sale Events
- See event details before sales open
- "Purchase" button shows **"Sales open on [date]"**
- Optionally indicate "Maybe" interest

### Sales Closed
- After sales deadline or sold out
- "Purchase" button shows **"Sales closed"**
- Contact organizer for late registration

## Privacy & Data

### What Organizers See
- Your name and email (from purchase)
- Participation status (Yes/Maybe/No)
- Attendance status (scanned or not)
- Participant details you provided

### Your Control
- Update participation status anytime
- Email used for ticket confirmation only
- Photo access tied to verified attendance
- Can request data deletion from platform

## Mobile Experience

### Best Practices
- Save ticket to phone's home screen for quick access
- Download ticket as PDF if printing
- Take screenshot of QR code as backup
- Enable push notifications for event updates

### Photo Gallery on Mobile
- Optimized for phone browsers
- Swipe between photos
- Tap to zoom
- Save photos to camera roll

## Support

### Common Questions
- **Lost ticket?** Check email or dashboard
- **Can't access photos?** Verify you were scanned at entrance
- **Wrong participant info?** Contact organizer to update
- **Want to change participation?** Toggle status on event page

### Contact Support
- Use in-app message system
- Include ticket UUID in support requests
- Response time: typically within 24 hours
